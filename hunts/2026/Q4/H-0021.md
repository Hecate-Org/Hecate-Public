The threat intelligence explicitly identifies CVE-2026-58115 as a critical unauthenticated Remote Code Execution (RCE) vulnerability in Siemens SIMATIC IoT2050 devices. Hunting for this specific behavior is critical because it represents an immediate, high-severity risk to industrial control systems (ICS) that may be exposed to the internet or internal networks without adequate segmentation or patching.
