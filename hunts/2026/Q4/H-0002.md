### Justification

Between April 8 and May 5, 2026, threat actors compromised the official software distribution pipeline of AVB Disc Soft. Official installers for DAEMON Tools Lite versions `12.5.0.2421` through `12.5.0.2434` were trojanized with backdoors embedded into `DTHelper.exe`, `DiscSoftBusServiceLite.exe`, and `DTShellHlp.exe`. Because these files carried legitimate developer Authenticode signatures, standard certificate-based prevention controls failed. Hunting across network egress and endpoint execution is vital to discover compromised hosts before secondary payloads execute.
